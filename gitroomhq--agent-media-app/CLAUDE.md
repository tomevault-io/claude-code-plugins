## Answer length

Do not over-explain. This is a standing instruction, not a preference.

- Lead with the outcome. One or two sentences.
- Say what the user has to do next, if anything.
- Stop.

Do not restate the reasoning, do not list what was verified, do not add caveats
that were not asked for. If something genuinely went wrong or a decision is
needed, say that in one line — everything else belongs in the commit message,
where it is retrievable and does not cost the reader anything.
